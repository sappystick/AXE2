Everything here is **MODELED / tool-checked**. Numbers come from the files named and the scripts cited. The PDFs are stored in `data/datasheets/`, `data/papers/` and `data/fab_guides/`.

## 2. What changed in AXE2 today

1. **MP1 v2 front end decided:** IXTY08N100D2 limiter (gate to the far end of 22 ohm), BAV199 clamps, then Rin2 = 100 ohm, then the LMH6629.
   - The `rf2_mp1_spice.py` section `v2_real_fets` models each FET from its own datasheet.
   - After the turn-off edge the limiter holds 101 mA.
   - **The edge itself** pushes C dV/dt through the FET's capacitance into the clamps for about 20 ns: 0.41-0.45 A (IXTY08N100D2) and 0.86-1.09 A (DN2470). This is an upper bound: the model releases the coil current instantly into 500 pF, about 16 V/ns. The real edge must be measured on the bench.
   - Rin2 = 100 ohm keeps the LMH6629 within its +-10 mA input limit while the clamp node sits at 6.55 V.
   - Front-end noise is about 1.7 nV/rtHz, against about 5.0 for v1, giving about **+23 % modeled mono depth**. Recovery is 14.7 us, inside the 16.3 us first gate; `run.py` step `mp1-spice` now checks this.
2. **Display hinge rules** (`rf2_ledgrid.hinge_check`, run step `display-hinge`), all passing:
   - no vias in the bend zone x' 28-34;
   - conductors cross the bend within 10 deg;
   - bend ratio 10:1 at today's 0.4 mm flex (exactly the minimum) and 26.7:1 as 0.15 mm PURE.flex;
   - finish: no nickel-containing finish at the bend.
3. **Thinner head study** (`rf2/rf2_thin.py`, results `rf2/results/rf2_thin.{json,png}`): see section 3.

**Reading:**
- **Same coil, flatter.** The 26-turn rim coil keeps its inductance and energy when it is re-shaped flatter and wider, and sitting lower brings it slightly closer to the ground.
- **No modeled depth loss.** In this model the head can go from 25.4 to about 12 mm without losing depth.

**Not modeled yet, needed before choosing:**
- **(a) The self-capacitance of the flatter bundle.** More turns sit side by side, so C may rise and move the first gate (study 2).
- **(b) The flex receive coils' thinner copper.** At 18 um against 35 um their resistance doubles (study 3).
- **(c) Clearance between the coil and AB1.** The coil's inner edge at r 103.2 is 1.2 mm from AB1, so eddy and pickup coupling of the coil into the analog boards needs checking.
- **(d) Mechanics.** A 12 mm shell's stiffness and the clear lid over a 233 mm span.
- **(e) Rule 6 at the new heights.** The HV coil leads and TXJ in a thinner HV sector still need >= 1.0 mm clearance.

**Suggested path:** do B first. It is a placement change and needs no new fabrication technology. Then C board by board, display first (already flex), then SH1, then the receive boards.

## 4. Winding and coil-geometry upgrades suggested by the sources

- **Litz for the rim coil** (Sci Rep reference: 36 AWG litz below 50 kHz). Specify the strand count against the AC resistance at the turn-off spectrum and the copper's own eddy decay (ENHANCEMENT_PLAN study 4).
- **Turn spacing and wire cross-section in L and M.** An 2022 shows that ignoring them gives inaccurate mutual inductance. `rf2_coil_search.L_turns` already includes the wire radius per turn; the flatter bundles of section 3 should be cross-checked with magpylib, with the wire as a finite cross-section.
- **Tilt and offset.** Their offset / tilt Neumann model, or magpylib with rotated coils, can quantify:
  - REF3 coupling under shaft tilt;
  - the quadrupole null against a board tilted by assembly tolerance (study 3);
  - target orientation effects.
- **Three-receiver idea.** Three coils 60 deg apart are their answer to orientation. In AXE2 the two quadrupole boards at 45 deg already give the full 180-deg-periodic bearing (cos 2 phi and sin 2 phi). A third board would add redundancy, not information, so it is not proposed.

## 5. Flex and rigid-flex process upgrades

- **Build options** (Wuerth): the display and the receive / shield layers as PURE.flex 2F, adhesiveless, about 0.1-0.15 mm. Use local stiffeners only under parts, and RIGID.flex (>= 0.8 mm) for the CB1D / AB1 islands with integral flex hinges.
- **Bends:**
  - radius >= 10 x thickness (1-2 layers), >= 20 x for 4 layers;
  - no vias and no stacked conductors in a bend;
  - conductors perpendicular to the bend;
  - no ENIG / ENEPIG on bend areas;
  - key-and-slot holding frames for RIGID.flex.
- **Fine features** (FCT UHDI): 0.05 / 0.05 mm lines and 0.08 mm laser vias would let the display cells shrink further, below the 8 mm grid, if the pinpoint model asks for finer rings. Today it uses 0.10 / 0.10 mm.
- **Fab drawing items** (for every flex): IPC-6013 class, bend zones and radii, coverlay openings, stiffener material and thickness, surface finish per zone, impedance (none needed today).
